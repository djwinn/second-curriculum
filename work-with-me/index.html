Work with me

The Second Curriculum is a six-month, one-to-one coaching programme for senior leaders who have stopped caring about work they are still very good at.

What it includes

  • Fortnightly ninety-minute sessions by video, for six months.
  • A short written reflection after every session, so nothing important gets lost between calls.
  • Messaging between sessions for the moments that cannot wait two weeks.

How it runs

Three movements: excavate what is already true, align it with the life you are living, then build the next chapter on purpose. Some clients change roles or start something of their own; just as many redesign the job they have so it connects to what they believe.

Start with a conversation

A first conversation is free and has no agenda. If it is a fit, we agree a start date. If it is not, you leave with a clearer question than you came with.
