The Second Curriculum

You did everything right. Twelve, fifteen, twenty years of doing it well. The title, the team, the salary, the respect of people you respect. And somewhere along the way the work went quiet inside you.

If that is where you are, you have not failed. You have graduated. The people who feel the most stuck are usually the ones who have outgrown their container, not the ones who have failed in it.

I coach senior leaders, mostly directors and vice presidents ten to twenty years into a corporate career, who look successful on paper and feel hollow in practice. They are good at their jobs. Often better than ever. They just stopped caring, and they cannot work out why, because nothing is technically wrong.

What I believe

  • Hollowness is information. It is not a mood to push through; it is the first honest signal you have had in years.
  • Your career is a curriculum. For the first decade you were a brilliant student. Now you have mastered the syllabus and nobody is handing you the next textbook.
  • The answer is rarely a promotion, a lateral move or another certification. It is almost always a question of alignment: are the things you build every day connected to what you believe matters?
  • The answers are already there. They are buried under years of being told what you are supposed to want.

How I work

We start with excavation. Before we talk about what is next, we find out what is true: the moments you lost track of time, the work you would do for free, the beliefs you stopped saying out loud. Then we test what we find against real life, in small experiments, not big leaps. Then you build the next chapter on purpose instead of by default.

I do not hand out answers. I ask better questions than you have been asked in a long time, and I stay with you while you answer them honestly.

Is this for you?

It is, if you are successful by every outside measure, you have started to suspect that the next promotion will not fix it, and you are ready to be honest about what you actually want, even if the answer is inconvenient.

Work with me: book a first conversation. There is no script. I just want to understand where you are and what has been pulling at you lately.
